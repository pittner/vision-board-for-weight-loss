# Weight Loss Vision Board 2027

36 weight loss and fitness goals in 6 groups (eating and nutrition, movement and training, habits and consistency, mindset and motivation, sleep and stress, tracking and milestones). Each goal has why it matters and a first step for this week, plus 12 honest questions and a 45-minute plan.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/vision-board-for-weight-loss/

- Press a goal and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=weightloss_2027) with the first step under it; save it as a 1080x1920 lock screen so you see it every time a craving hits
- Or type your own goal and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
